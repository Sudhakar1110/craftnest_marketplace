About CraftNest

Connecting artisans with the world, one handcrafted piece at a time.

Our Story

CraftNest was born from a simple belief: every artisan deserves a platform to showcase their craft to the world. We connect talented craftspeople from rural India with customers who appreciate the value of handcrafted goods.

Our platform provides artisans with the tools and reach they need to grow their businesses, while giving customers access to unique, authentic handcrafted products that tell a story.

Our Mission

Preserve Traditions

We work to preserve traditional craftsmanship and support artisans in continuing their heritage skills.

Empower Artisans

By providing a marketplace and business tools, we help artisans grow their businesses and reach new customers.

Sustainable Future

We promote sustainable practices and eco-friendly products that are good for the planet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a seller?

Click on "Become a Seller" and complete the registration process. Our team will review your application within 2-3 business days.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, UPI, Wallets, and Cash on Delivery.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy on all products. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

How long does shipping take?

Processing time is 3-5 days, and shipping takes an additional 3-7 days depending on your location.
